The Brief

Himchi is a year-old Indo-Chinese café and street food brand in Delhi targeting urban millennials and Gen Z (18–32). The founder's brief was open-ended: "Make it look great, make it mobile-friendly, and make sure people can reserve a table easily."

This was a graded IIMB assignment for the Digital Marketing & Consumer Behaviour course. I worked solo — responsible for the full project from client interview to live deployment and go-to-market execution.

The Challenge

Delhi's F&B sector is one of the most digitally competitive in India. A website for a brand like Himchi needs to function as a conversion channel, a brand trust signal, and a social asset — not just an information page.

With the large majority of Indian web traffic now on mobile, a mobile-first approach wasn't optional. It was the only viable approach.

What I Built

01
Glassmorphism UI with Steam-Particle Effects

A layered, translucent visual language paired with ambient steam-particle animation — reinforcing the food-and-warmth identity without slowing the page down.

02
Mobile-First Responsive Design

Multi-breakpoint system with fluid typography via clamp(), and touch-optimised elements across all devices.

03
WhatsApp Reservation Funnel

A WhatsApp-based reservation flow, reducing friction versus a traditional web form and meeting customers on the channel they already use.

04
Swiggy & Zomato Deep Links

Direct links to Himchi's actual Swiggy and Zomato listings — turning the website into a real conversion hub for online orders.

05
GTM Execution

Beyond the build, ran the go-to-market motion connecting the new site to the brand's existing delivery and discovery channels.
