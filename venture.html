~66% ROI  ·  ~40% Profit Margin  ·  30-Minute Execution

The Challenge

Build a real product and sell it — with exactly ₹250 in capital and a 30-minute selling window. No safety net. No extensions. Real customers, real money, real stakes.

This was an IIM Bangalore exercise designed to test rapid ideation, market validation, and execution under extreme constraints.

The Idea

Using the SCAMPER framework, I identified an opportunity in custom pop-culture metro card sleeves — a low-cost, highly shareable product targeting Delhi metro commuters and students who personalise their everyday items.

The pop-culture angle was deliberate: it creates an instant emotional connection and reduces the "do I need this?" hesitation. People buy things they identify with.

Execution

01
Product Design

Designed sleeves with pop-culture references relevant to the target audience — students and young professionals.

02
Pricing Strategy

Priced to maximise margin while staying in impulse-buy territory. Tested different price points in the first few minutes and locked in the optimal one.

03
Dual-Channel Selling

Ran two parallel channels simultaneously — offline (in-person) and WhatsApp — to maximise reach within the 30-minute window.

04
Live Demand Validation

Real-time feedback from buyers informed on-the-spot adjustments to pitch and product positioning.

Key Takeaway

Speed and clarity beat perfection. A simple product with a clear emotional hook, priced right, and sold through two channels simultaneously can generate meaningful returns even in an extreme constraint environment. The SCAMPER framework isn't just theoretical — it works in real-time.
