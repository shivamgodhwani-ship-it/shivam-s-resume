Pixels & Profits

Inter-College Case Exercise  ·  IIM Bangalore BBA DBE  ·  2025

Best Human Connection Award  ·  Featured on IIM Bangalore's Official Instagram

What Was It?

Pixels & Profits was an inter-college business growth case exercise held at IIM Bangalore as part of the BBA Digital Business & Entrepreneurship program. Participants were given a real business scenario and tasked with diagnosing the core problem and presenting a structured solution.

The Problem

The case involved a business facing stagnating growth. The surface-level symptoms pointed to operational inefficiencies — but most teams got stuck at that layer. The real challenge was identifying the deeper cause: a breakdown in trust and emotional connection with users.

My Approach

01
Reframed the Problem

Rather than jumping to operational fixes, I stepped back and asked why users were disengaged in the first place. The root issue was not process — it was perception and trust.

02
Identified the Behavioural Gap

Users weren't connecting with the brand emotionally. The solution needed to address how people felt, not just what they experienced functionally.

03
Built a Human-Centric Strategy

I developed a framework around trust-building mechanisms, empathy-driven communication, and problem framing from the user's perspective rather than the business's.

04
Structured Communication

The delivery focused on clarity — breaking down the insight and solution in a way that was intuitive, well-argued, and human. No jargon, just substance.

Key Takeaway

The most effective business strategies aren't always the most complex. Often, the biggest lever is understanding how people feel — and building your solution around that. Structured thinking works best when it's grounded in human empathy.
