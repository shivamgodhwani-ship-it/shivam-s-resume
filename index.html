About

I'm a first-year student pursuing a dual academic journey at IIM Bangalore (BBA — Digital Business & Entrepreneurship) and Shyam Lal College, University of Delhi (BA Economics). I'm originally from Katni, Madhya Pradesh — transitioning to Delhi and IIMB has shaped how I think, adapt, and communicate.

My interests sit at the intersection of business strategy, psychology, and human behaviour. I'm curious about how people make decisions and how that can be applied to build practical, user-centric solutions — whether that's a competition case, a live venture, or a real website for a local business.

I focus on combining structured thinking with execution. I don't just ideate — I build and ship.
