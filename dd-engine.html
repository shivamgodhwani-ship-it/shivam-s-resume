AI-Powered Commercial Due Diligence Tool  ·  Solo Build  ·  2026

Company name in  ·  structured investment brief out in under 60 seconds

The Problem

Early-stage commercial due diligence usually starts the same way: an analyst spends an hour pulling together basic facts about a company before any real judgement work can begin — what it does, who runs it, how risky it looks, where the red flags are.

That hour is repetitive and structured enough to automate, but it still needs to produce something an analyst would actually trust and use.

What I Built

01
Single-Input Pipeline

The entire tool runs from one input — a company name — and produces a complete brief without further manual steps.

02
Structured Investment Brief

Output includes a verdict, a risk score, a five-dimension scorecard, and a clearly separated list of red flags and opportunities — not a wall of generated text.

03
LLM Reasoning Layer

Uses LLaMA 3.3 70B via the Groq API for fast inference, structuring the model's reasoning into the fixed brief format rather than free-form output.

04
Analyst-Ready Output

Final documents are generated with ReportLab, designed specifically for readability — built to be handed to an analyst, not just read on screen.

Design Decisions

Speed and structure mattered more than exhaustive depth. A brief that takes 60 seconds and gives a clear verdict is more useful at the screening stage than a slower tool that produces unstructured analysis.

The tool is deployable two ways — as a standalone CLI tool for quick individual lookups, or as an API endpoint for integration into a larger workflow.

Outcome

Stack

Key Takeaway

The hardest part of applying an LLM to analyst work isn't getting a good answer out of the model — it's forcing that answer into a structure someone can actually act on under time pressure.
