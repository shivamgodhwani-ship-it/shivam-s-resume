The Brief

Himchi is a year-old Indo-Chinese café and street food brand in Delhi targeting urban millennials and Gen Z (18–32). The founder's brief was open-ended: "Make it look great, make it mobile-friendly, and make sure people can reserve a table easily."

This was a graded IIMB assignment for the Digital Marketing & Consumer Behaviour course. I worked solo — responsible for the full project from client interview to live deployment.

The Challenge

Delhi's F&B sector is one of the most digitally competitive in India. A well-designed website for a brand like Himchi needs to function as a conversion channel, a brand trust signal, and a social sharing asset — not just an information page.

With 79.3% of Indian web traffic on mobile (Statcounter, Q1 2026), a mobile-first approach wasn't optional. It was the only viable approach.

What I Built

01
Mobile-First Responsive Design

4-breakpoint system (900px/820px/540px/360px), fluid typography via clamp(), and touch-optimised elements across all devices.

02
WhatsApp Reservation Flow

Integrated a WhatsApp-based reservation system — leveraging India's 534M+ WhatsApp users and reducing friction vs. traditional web forms.

03
Swiggy & Zomato Integration

Direct links to Himchi's actual Swiggy and Zomato listings — turning the website into a real conversion hub for online orders.

04
Animated Brand Mascot

Section-aware mascot expressions that change as users scroll — hungry eyes on menu, heart eyes at contact. Micro-delight without cognitive load.

05
Dark Mode with Persistence

82% of smartphone users prefer dark mode, especially during 8–10 PM — peak reservation intent. Preference persists across sessions.

06
Ambient Video & Carousel Menu

Non-interactive looping video for ambience. Auto-scrolling carousel replacing a static menu grid — increasing item visibility on mobile.

Projected Impact

📉
Mobile Bounce Rate

Projected reduction from ~68% to ~42%

📈
Reservation Conversion

Projected increase from ~1.2% to ~3.8% (~217% improvement)

⏱️
Session Duration

Projected +45–60 seconds per mobile session

Design System

Primary accent: #F3703A (warm terracotta orange) — dual resonance with Indo-Chinese spice and WCAG AA accessibility. Dark background palette increases perceived product premiumness and enhances food photography appeal.

Typography: Fraunces (display/brand) + DM Sans (body/UI). All animations follow Material Design 3 purposeful motion principles.

Key Takeaway

A real client with real business goals changes everything. The brief wasn't to build a nice website — it was to build one that converts visitors into reservations and online orders. Every design decision was made in service of that commercial objective, grounded in consumer behaviour research.
