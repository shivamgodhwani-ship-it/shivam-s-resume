About

I'm a first-year student pursuing a dual academic path — BBA in Digital Business & Entrepreneurship at IIM Bangalore, and BA Economics & English at Shyam Lal College, University of Delhi. I'm originally from Katni, Madhya Pradesh; the move to Delhi and then IIMB has shaped how I think, adapt, and communicate.

My interests sit at the intersection of strategy, behavioural research, and execution. I'm equally comfortable framing a business case, analysing a 35-year government dataset, or shipping a Python pipeline — what connects them is a habit of asking what's actually driving the outcome, then building something that acts on the answer.

I don't just ideate. I build, test, and ship — whether that's a live website for a real café, a research metric, or a tool that automates its own use.
